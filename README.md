**Prep Time:** 10–15 minutes

**Oven Temperature:** 350°F (175°C)

## 🪄 Wanda's Secret Fairy Cookie Tips

**Wanda:** "Listen carefully! These little tricks make all the difference!"

### 🍪 For Extra Chewy Cookies

Use mostly brown sugar, just like our recipe. Brown sugar helps keep the centers soft and chewy.

### 🍪 For Thick Cookies

Refrigerate the dough for **20–30 minutes** before baking. This helps prevent excessive spreading.

### 🍪 For Gooey Centers

Remove the cookies when the edges are set but the middles are still soft.

They'll finish setting on the baking sheet!

### 🍪 For Extra Chocolatey Cookies

Press a few additional chocolate chips into the tops of the dough balls before baking.

### 🍪 For Storing Leftovers

Once completely cooled, store the cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for about **3–4 days**.
